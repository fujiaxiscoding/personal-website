## Overview

Reconnect is an AI-guided journaling companion that helps people notice and process difficult emotions in the moment, rather than months later in therapy. I led product design end-to-end, from early research through a public beta launch.

## The problem

Most emotional wellness apps ask people to journal into a vacuum — a blank page with no guidance, no memory of what came before, and no sense of progress. Early interviews with 14 users revealed the real barrier wasn't willingness to reflect, it was not knowing how to start, and feeling like the effort disappeared into the void.

## Process

<ProcessStep number="01" title="Understanding the block">
Through diary studies and contextual interviews, I mapped the emotional and practical friction points that kept people from opening the app, even when they wanted to reflect.
</ProcessStep>

<ProcessStep number="02" title="Prototyping conversation, not forms">
Instead of static prompts, I designed a lightweight conversational flow that adapts its questions based on what someone has already shared, tested across six rounds of prototypes.
</ProcessStep>

<ProcessStep number="03" title="Designing for return, not just entry">
The biggest design challenge wasn't the first session, it was the tenth. I designed a gentle "threads" view that resurfaces themes over time without feeling like surveillance.
</ProcessStep>

## Outcome

Reconnect launched to a 500-person beta with a 61% four-week retention rate, well above the category benchmark. Users described the experience as "the first journaling app that actually remembers me."

## Reflection

This project sharpened how I think about AI products: the interface isn't the chat window, it's the trust built session over session. The most important design decisions were about pacing and memory, not visual polish.
