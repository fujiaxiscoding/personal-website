04What I found
I thoughtPeople needed better recommendations.
I lookedI watched how people pick a place once they’re already out and about.
I realizedWhat they mostly needed was help narrowing things down.
1Travelers combine several tools to make one small decision.
2When people saw more options, they usually felt less sure about which one to pick.
3Knowing the context can rule out most places before any recommendation starts.
05Key insight
The more Vagus understands the moment, the fewer choices it needs to show.

09Key design decisions
01Context before categories
InsightTravel decisions depend on the situation you’re in.
In the UIHome opens on the current moment, with no list of restaurants and attractions to sort through.
02A few directions to choose from
InsightPeople still want to make the final call themselves.
In the UIVagus shows three directions, and the traveler picks the one that suits them.
03Refine by reacting
InsightPeople often know what they don’t want before they know what they do.
In the UICloser, Quieter, More local and Less walking re-sort the list with one tap.
04Chat that knows the context
InsightNobody wants to explain where they are every time they ask something.
In the UIAsk Vagus picks up the place and the moment from whatever screen you came from.
05Technology steps back
InsightThe point is to get the traveler back into the place they’re in.
In the UIAfter a decision, the screen shrinks to the destination, the walk and one button.

16How I’d measure it
Success means people decide with less effort and still feel the decision was their own.
01Decision timeHow long from opening the app to tapping Go there.
02Decision confidenceA quick check afterwards: did that feel like the right call?
03Refinement usageHow often people tap Closer or Quieter before they choose.
04Follow-throughWhether people actually go to the place they picked.
05Search abandonmentHow often someone leaves without picking anything.
06Return through Field NotesWhether people come back to look at what they kept.
07Helped me decideDid Vagus help them decide, or did it feel like Vagus decided for them?
