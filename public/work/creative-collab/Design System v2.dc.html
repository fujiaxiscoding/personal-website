Design System · v2

Creative Collab Design System

The system gives a creative product enough structure to support clear decisions and collaboration without flattening its personality.

Built as a semantic Tailwind theme on shadcn/ui primitives: one variant × size × state matrix per component, shared by design and engineering.
00

Product rationale

Creative Collab is designed to make collaboration feel like an invitation.

Inspired by the social energy of platforms like Partiful and Luma, the system balances expressiveness with structure: playful enough to spark participation, but clear enough to support real creative commitments. Every interaction should move the user from “that looks interesting” to “I want to be part of this” with as little friction as possible.

Principles

01 Participation over administration
Make joining and contributing feel lightweight. Reduce the feeling of forms, applications, and workflow management.
02 Energy creates participation
Use expressive typography, warm accents, and strong visual moments to make projects feel alive and worth entering.
03 Playful to enter. Serious enough to commit.
Keep discovery social and low-pressure, while making project details, roles, states, and commitments clear when decisions matter.

Brand & logo

One mark, one wordmark, one lockup. The mark is a coral tile with the spark knocked out of it — the spark is never drawn as a positive shape.
Mark · knockout tile
Spark sits at 86% of the tile, optically centred. Tile radius tracks size: 26px at 116, 18px at 72, 11px at 44, 6px at 26. Never below 20px.
Wordmark · nudged baseline
CreativeCollab
Bricolage Grotesque 800, -0.02em. Per-letter vertical nudge of ±2px at 30px, scaled linearly with size. The second C is primary; every other letter is foreground.
Lockup · horizontal
CreativeCollab
CreativeCollab
Gap = mark ÷ 3. On coral the mark inverts (ink spark on coral tile) and the wordmark drops the nudge and the coral letter — it reads as one flat ink unit. This is the footer signature.
Mark variants
coral · default
cream on coral
one ink
Coral on ink is the default. Cream on coral is for coral grounds; one-ink is for single-colour print, favicons and anywhere coral would fail contrast. No fourth variant.
Rules — clearspace equals the tile's corner radius on all sides. The mark never carries a gradient, a stroke, or a rotation. On photography use the coral tile, never the wordmark alone. The launch-screen glow (shadow-brand-bloom) is the only shadow the logo is ever allowed.

Radius & elevation

Radius scales with the surface: 10 chip, 16 input, 24 card, 26 sheet, full for buttons and tags. Elevation is structural — a card carries its letterpress at rest.

A card is pressed into the page, not floated above it. shadow-card stacks five layers: a warm 1px inset highlight on top, a dark inset underneath, a 2px black ring, a close ambient blur, and one long soft drop. Hover strengthens the highlight and adds a coral outer glow at 30%; it never changes geometry. Only sheets and dialogs use neutral black, because they separate planes rather than raise an object. shadow-brand-bloom is reserved for the logo at launch size and decays to zero as the mark shrinks into the header — a shadow that stays behind a 26px mark reads as blur.

Motion

Fast120ms
Hover, press, chip select, checkbox fill. Feels like the control, not an animation of it.
Standard240ms
Toast in/out, dropdown, tab swap, skeleton → content crossfade.
Slow300ms
Sheet rise, dialog scale 0.96→1, screen fade. The only noticeable motion.
Cards hover with shadow and border only — the geometry never moves, because a grid that shifts is harder to scan. A tappable card presses to 0.985, a button to 0.98, both with no bounce. Toasts are glass pills that fade + rise 8px in and fade only out.
Spinner 0.9s linear, constant — it reports duration. Skeleton pulses at 1.6s, no travelling shimmer. Under prefers-reduced-motion transitions cut to instant and the pulse stops; spinners keep turning.

Launch sequence

2400ms, one continuous move: the logo assembles at centre, then travels to its resting place in the feed header. Nothing else animates. Tapping anywhere skips to the feed.
0 – 24% · mark in
Tile scales 0.3 → 1 and fades in on cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1), carrying shadow-brand-bloom.
22 – 54% · wordmark
Letters cascade in at 34ms stagger, 420ms each, rising 9px. Held still until the move begins.
54 – 100% · travel
Mark translates to the header slot and scales to 0.259 (116px → 30px) while the bloom decays to zero. The centred wordmark fades out as the header lockup takes over.
58 – 100% · curtain
The dark launch plane slides up and off on cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1), revealing the feed already in place. No leading edge, no light streak — the plane leaves silently.
The launch screen is a transition, not a splash. It exists to move the logo into the header, so the destination is visible the moment the plane clears. Nothing loads behind it and no progress is implied. Under prefers-reduced-motion the whole sequence resolves instantly to the feed.

Accessibility

Touch targets
44×44 minimum, including the save heart over a cover and the ✕ on a tag. Visual size may be smaller; the hit area is not.
Keyboard & focus
DOM order matches reading order; feed cards are one tab stop. Overlays trap focus and return it to the trigger. Esc always closes. Focus ring is coral, never removed.
Colour independence
Error ⚠ + text · success ✓ + text · selected ✓ + border · status badges are words. Every state survives greyscale.
Labels & announcements
Icon buttons name action and object — "Save Experimental Dance Film". Loading text goes through a polite live region so it's heard, not just seen. Errors use aria-invalid + aria-describedby.
Contrast
Body ≥4.5:1, large text and borders ≥3:1 — which is why the card border sits at coral 60% rather than the prettier 30%.
Reduced motion
Transitions cut to instant, sheets appear without travel, skeleton pulse stops. Spinners keep turning — they carry information.
